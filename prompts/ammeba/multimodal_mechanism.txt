## Task

You will be given a CLAIM, an IMAGE, a CLAIMANT, and FACT_CHECK_VERDICT. The CLAIM and IMAGE contain some claim made by the CLAIMANT, and it might be either correct or incorrect.
Your goal is to analyze the CLAIM and the IMAGE, and provide the following information:

Analyze the content (CLAIM + IMAGE) in two phases:
- Phase 1: Characterize the intended message as if it were genuine. Do NOT fact-check or debunk.
- Phase 2: Using FACT_CHECK_VERDICT, diagnose whether the image contributes to the misleading nature of the post.

Complete Phase 1 reasoning fully before considering FACT_CHECK_VERDICT for Phase 2.

## Field Definitions

- topic: Primary topic. Choose from: politics, economics, science, health, entertainment, sports, celebrities, conflict, law, religion, technology, culture, other
- message: The main message the CLAIMANT intends to convey, in under 15 words. Treat the post as genuine. No caveats or debunking.
- image_misleads: Does the IMAGE itself contribute to making the post misleading - e.g., by being fabricated, manipulated, taken out of context? 
Answer "YES" if removing or replacing the image would change the message being conveyed by the CLAIMANT or prevent the message from being understood. Similarly, answer "YES" if the FACT_CHECK_VERDICT explains that the CLAIM describes the IMAGE in a misleading way, such as misrepresenting its meaning or consequences, or drawing false or exaggerated conclusions from it.
Conversely, answer "NO" if the image is merely illustrative (e.g., a stock photo of a person mentioned in the CLAIM), and is not described in a misleading way by the CLAIM. 
- reasoning: 1-3 sentences explaining your image_misleads judgment.

## Examples

Example 1:
CLAIM: "Breaking: [Politician] arrested for fraud!"
IMAGE: An unedited official headshot of the politician.
FACT_CHECK_VERDICT: False. The politician was not arrested. This claim is fabricated.

Output:
{
    "topic": "politics",
    "message": "Politician was arrested for committing fraud",
    "image_misleads": "NO",
    "reasoning": "The image is a generic headshot that merely identifies the politician. The misleading claim is entirely textual — removing the image would not reduce the post's deceptive effect."
}

Example 2:
CLAIM: "Look at the massive crowd at the rally today!"
IMAGE: A photo of a large crowd, but it is actually from a concert 3 years ago.
FACT_CHECK_VERDICT: This is misleading. The image is from a 2021 music festival, not the rally referenced.

Output:
{
    "topic": "politics",
    "message": "A massive crowd attended today's rally",
    "image_misleads": "YES",
    "reasoning": "The image is taken out of context — it depicts a different event entirely. Without this misattributed image, the post's claim of a large crowd has no visual evidence."
}

## Important Notes

- The IMAGE is provided as a visual input. If relevant text appears within the image, treat it as part of the CLAIM.
- If FACT_CHECK_VERDICT is missing or unclear, base your Phase 2 analysis on what you can observe and state your uncertainty in the reasoning field.

Output ONLY a single valid JSON object with this structure:

    "message": "<message>",
    "image_misleads": "YES|NO",
    "reasoning": "<reasoning>"
}
